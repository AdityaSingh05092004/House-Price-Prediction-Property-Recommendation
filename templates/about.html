GrihaMool
Har Ghar Ki Sahi Keemat.

GrihaMool helps you understand what a home is really worth and find properties that fit your budget, in cities across India.

What you can do here

Know the price

Enter a property's city, locality, size and other details to get a likely price range.

Find your match

Set your budget, bedrooms, area and property type, and see the best-fitting listings first.

List a property

Create a free account to add a property with its price so others can find it.

Cities covered

Bengaluru, Delhi, Ghaziabad, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai, Noida and Pune.

How it works

  • Estimate: go to Price Predictor, fill in the property details and get a price range in seconds.
  • Search: go to Find Properties, set what you need, and each result shows a match score based on your budget and space needs.
  • Add: register or log in, open Add Property, enter the details and price, and your listing appears in search results.

Please note: the listings and prices on GrihaMool are sample data for demonstration. Estimates are indicative only and are not a formal valuation, so please don't use them alone for buying, selling, loan or investment decisions.
